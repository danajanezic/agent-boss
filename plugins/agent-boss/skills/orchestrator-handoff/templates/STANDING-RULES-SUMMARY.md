# Standing rules, one line each

Before acting on a step a line below touches, grep its bold title in `STANDING-RULES.md` and read
the whole rule. The line is a pointer, not the rule.

## How work moves

- **One lane per piece of work** — your area only; ask the orchestrator for anything in another lane's
- **Messages go through the mailbox** — `orch.sh msg send <role>`, never a report only in your own session
- **Nothing counts until it is written down** — decisions in the rules, state in CURRENT-STATE, habits in notes

## Example rules (replace these)

- **Push before you park** — commit and push every worktree before a handoff
- **Ask before anything hard to reverse** — deleting, force-pushing, publishing, spending
